- Recommended sampling: **temp=0.7 + top_p=0.85** balances diversity and coherence; **temp=0.6** is most stable; **greedy** easily falls into repetition
- English novels: 1.5B frequently produces RWKV vocab's built-in `✿reverse_word...✿` meta-markers, unrelated to quantization, this is the ceiling of the small model's English ability
